What this bot is

A starter crypto trading bot provided by ANDX. It comes with proven default strategies, but it's a base to build on — you're meant to change the settings and edit the code to make it trade your way. It runs on your own computer and starts on practice money so you can experiment with zero risk.

Top bar

Start botTurns the bot on. It begins reading the market and trading. When running, this button becomes Stop.
StopPauses the bot. It stops making new trades but keeps any positions it's holding.
Stop & close allStops the bot and sells everything at market — the emergency "get me out" button.
⚡ DerivativesON: trade long and short with leverage (bet up or down). OFF: spot only — buy coins, sell back to cash. Switching closes open positions first.
🛡️ ConservativeCalmer, profit-focused mode: fewer, stronger trades, smaller risk each, wider stops, a daily-loss circuit breaker.

Settings explained

Trading modePaper = practice money (safe, start here). Live = real money with your own keys.
StrategyThe playbook. Auto picks trend-following or mean-reversion based on the market; the others force one style.
SymbolsWhich coins to trade. ALL = every ANDX pair, or list your own.
TimeframeHow zoomed-in the bot reads charts. 15m = fast & active; 1d = slow & patient.
Reset practice accountStarts your practice balance fresh.

Risk controls (your safety limits)

Risk per trade %Most of the account one losing trade can cost. 1% = a loss can't hurt more than 1%.
ATR stop ×Where the auto "get-out" price sits. ATR is a coin's normal daily wiggle; this places the stop that many wiggles away, so normal noise doesn't kick you out but a real move does.
Max leverageHow much borrowed money it may use (derivatives). Multiplies wins and losses.
Max positionsMost coins it can hold at once.
Max position % of equityMost it can put into any single coin.
Daily loss kill switch %Lose this much in one day and the bot sells everything and stops until tomorrow.

Going live & safety

Start in paper mode. Learn and test with practice money first. When you're ready for real money: create API keys in your own ANDX account (trade permission only, never withdrawals), paste them in Settings → Save & test keys, switch Trading mode to Live, and confirm.

Your keys are stored only on your computer and are never uploaded. Crypto trading — especially leveraged and short — can lose money fast. Start small.

Exchange & API keys

The bot trades ANDX derivatives — real longs and shorts with leverage (set by the "Max leverage" risk field), and stop-loss/take-profit enforced by the exchange itself. Candles, quotes, orders, and balances all come from ANDX's API; only the key and secret are needed.
Keys are stored only on this machine (secrets.json, owner-read-only) and never leave it except to call your exchange. Create keys with trade permission only — never enable withdrawals.

Bot settings

Edit Strategy — your bot's brain

This is strategies.py, where the buy/sell decisions live. Change the logic, hit Save & Apply, and your bot runs your code. It's checked for errors first — broken code is refused, so you can't brick your bot. A good place to start: the numbers in EMATrend (fast/slow moving averages) or RSIMeanReversion (the RSI buy/sell levels). Test in paper mode.
